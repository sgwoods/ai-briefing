2. Google releases HARNESST2 recursive agent framework

Marktechpost · source · source · 2026-10-18

  • What happened: Google Cloud AI Research open-sourced Harness² (v0.1.0), which edits an agent's harness (prompts, tools, memory) at test time with weights frozen. The paper reports one refinement step improved all 4 setups on 3 benchmarks, up to +11.5 points.
  • Evidence check: Overstated. Paper's own benchmark gains; a frontier editor and extra runs per task, not just harness edits, may explain them.
  • Our read: This helps teams shipping agents on frozen frontier models: automated harness search may become standard tooling, and it squeezes startups whose edge is hand-tuned prompts. We would want per-task cost and run counts before believing +11.5 points transfers to production.
  • The AGI-forward view: An agent improving its own harness at test time, weights frozen, is a path to compounding capability without retraining; one refinement step lifting all 4 setups is an early sign.
