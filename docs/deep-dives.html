Topical deep dives

A separate episode of about ten minutes (deepdive.minutes 10, up to max_minutes 13 when an important story needs the context), also published as a readable article on its own page (site: &lt;day>/deep-dive/, and the front page's Deep dives section), after the daily briefing, on the day's focal point: a breakthrough and its controversy, a concept suddenly everywhere (the "third scaling law": scaling compute at inference time), a change in the trajectory of the field, or a story that has matured over several days.

  • Who decides: Claude picks the topic from today's briefing, the past week and recent deep dives, and may say there is none today. One a day (deepdive.max_per_week 7, since 2026-10-19), no repeat within 21 days unless something material is new. ./np deepdive --topic "..." sets the topic yourself.
  • How it is written: an explainer for the ear, in six parts: why today → the idea plainly → what is established vs claimed → how the AGI-forward / AI-realist / market / policy readings differ (only where the facts allow) → our read and one diligence question → what would settle it. Claude may search the web for the paper, the lab's post and the strongest critique.
  • Checks before it airs: every source link is opened (dead ones dropped; fewer than 2 live → no episode); quotation marks survive only around words found in the archived or fetched text; sentences with a $ or % figure found in no source are cut; private names are removed; markup or banned words fail the episode. The summary and "Why today" lines are then checked against the finished body (pipeline/consistency.py, since v0.1.9): a sentence whose figure, company or "not disclosed" claim the body doesn't support is dropped. See docs/FACT-CHECKING.md.
  • In the podcast (2026-10-19): the deep dive follows the briefing in the same episode. The briefing host ("I'm Michael" in the first line) closes by promoting today's deep dive and where to find it (the web page, with the written edition and every past episode), hands off to Bella, and the deep dive's sign-off closes the episode. The feed carries one file per day (briefings/&lt;day>.podcast.mp3, "… · with deep dive: <title>"); the web page keeps separate players for the briefing and the deep dive. If the briefing audio was made without the hand-off (a deep dive made later by hand), the deep dive stays a separate bonus episode.
  • Where it goes: briefings/deepdives/<day>.md (show notes + sources), .script.md, .mp3. The podcast feed lists it as a bonus episode "Deep dive: <title>" right after that day's briefing; the day's page links to it.
  • When: planned during the morning run before the briefing audio (so the briefing hands off to it), written and voiced right after, then published and mentioned in the email.
  • Picking from the evening note: your private note (sent by the daily run after the subscriber email) lists the suggestions with a one-click "Make this next" for each, and a link to a private queue page to reorder, dismiss or suggest a topic (signed owner links, 8-day expiry).
  • Queue: dashboard /deep-dives: Claude adds up to 4 suggestions each morning; queue, reorder, dismiss or add your own. Up next goes first; otherwise the day's best (strength 4+). One a day; Up next is never left empty (the best suggestion is moved up if you queued nothing).
  • Voices: briefing am_michael ("Michael", ~131 words a minute), deep dive af_bella ("Bella", ~157), changeable on the Tuning page. Script length is set from each voice's measured speed (audio.voice_wpm).
