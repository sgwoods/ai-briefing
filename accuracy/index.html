Accuracy

Before each edition is sent, every factual claim in its top section, What happened lines and Worth knowing is checked against the sources it came from; a line that is only partly right or contradicted is rewritten to match them (citing a new source, government and regulators first, when the cited ones lack the fact), then checked again by a second, different model. The time of that check is noted at the end of each edition. After the edition goes out, the second model checks about five of its factual claims again against the sources they came from: supported, partly right, not found in those sources, or contradicted by them. The checks also count places where the edition disagrees with itself or with the last two weeks. Anything partly right or contradicted goes to Steven, who decides whether to correct it. The checks are machine-run, and the corrections below are made by hand.
