3. NVIDIA releases Nemotron 3.5 Super VL and Bolt models for edge deployment

NVIDIA (blog) · primary source · source · 2026-10-15

  • What happened: NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Super VL (120B open model) with frontier accuracy at 1/5 size of larger models, plus two edge-optimized Bolt models: Bolt 74B-A9B (24GB memory) and Bolt E4B (4GB). All cover agent deployments from enterprise to edge.
  • Agenda: NVIDIA blog announcing its own product; sells chips
  • Our read: Open 120B and 4GB-class models from the chip vendor may help vertical agent startups that need on-device or on-prem deployment without frontier API bills. We would want independent evals before accepting "frontier accuracy", a claim from the GPU seller.
  • The market view: NVIDIA gives away capable open models because cheaper models mean more deployments on its hardware; commoditising the model layer pressures labs that price by API.
