2. Cohere researchers report mixed agent teams cut ProgramBench costs 70%

Cohere / Cohere Labs · primary source · source

  • What happened: Cohere reports a heterogeneous agent team (1 Sol lead + 16 Luna planning workers) achieved 61.8% reward on ProgramBench at $155 cost, vs. all-Sol's 63.2% at $510 ($70 cheaper; 1.4-point drop), per Cohere Labs research (Cohere). The team beat solo agents but cost 3–7x more than homogeneous teams (Cohere Research Paper).
  • Evidence check: Holds. Self-reported, one benchmark; arithmetic checks out for cost savings vs. reward trade-off.
  • Our read: Using a strong model to lead cheap workers is a real design opening for agent startups. Ask every agent founder for cost per solved task across model mixes. One benchmark is not enough to set pricing.
